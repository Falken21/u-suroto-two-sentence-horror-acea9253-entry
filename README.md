A two-sentence horror micro-story, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The story

> Every photo my phone takes of the nursery comes out blurred in one corner, as if something moved just as the shutter closed. Last night I scrolled back through a year of them and saw the blur a little closer to the crib in every picture — and in the first, from the day we brought her home, it is tall and standing in the doorway, facing the camera.

Exactly two sentences, 349 characters — inside the competition's 400-character limit.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This creates `/output/` if needed and writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`
as UTF-8 text with no trailing newline. Python 3 standard library only;
nothing to install.
